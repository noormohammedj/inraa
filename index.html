Result as a Service, not Software as a Service

Most software companies stop when you log in. Inraa is measured by what changes in your business afterwards.

SaaS: software as a service
  • You buy licences and learn a new tool
  • You set it up, move your data and train staff
  • You chase your team to actually use it
  • You pay every month, whether it helped or not
  • Problems are a support ticket
RaaS: result as a service · Inraa
  • We agree the result you want, in numbers
  • We set it up, move your data and train your team
  • We automate the work, so nobody has to remember it
  • We report the result every month and improve it
  • Problems are ours to fix, the same day
